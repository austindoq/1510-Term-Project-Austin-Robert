After a vicious battle between the forces of Francisco de Orellana and the Omagua people... 
Your troop of Spanish conquistadors returns to their river boats to continue on their voyage down the Amazon river.
Before finding a solid place to set up camp for the battalion of 80 men, a flash storm throws your boat down a smaller, branching river. 
You stand alone on the river shore, dripping wet and seemingly the only one that survived being thrown into the rapids.
Maybe if you can follow this smaller river back to the Amazon by foot you can find and rejoin your group.
